Trail Map: Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

Trail Map is a browser extension that shows the path of pages you followed in a tab as a short breadcrumb and lets you jump back to any step. This policy explains what information it handles. The short version: everything stays on your device, and nothing is sent to the developer or anyone else.

What Trail Map handles

To build the breadcrumb, the extension reads, for each tab you browse in:

  • the web address (URL) of each page you visit,
  • the title of each page, and
  • how you reached it (for example by following a link, typing an address, or using Back).

This is web browsing history. It is used only to draw the breadcrumb and to take you back to a step when you click it.

Where it is stored

  • Trails are kept in your browser's temporary session storage (chrome.storage.session). They are erased when you close the browser, when you close the tab, or when you clear them.
  • Your settings (whether tracking is paused and which sites you excluded) are kept in your browser's local extension storage until you change them or remove the extension.
  • Each tab's trail holds at most 100 pages. The oldest are dropped first.

What is never done with your data

  • It is not sent to the developer or to any server. The extension makes no network requests.
  • It is not sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.
  • It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose other than the breadcrumb feature.
  • The extension contains no analytics, tracking code, or remotely loaded code.
  • Site icons are loaded from your browser's own local favicon cache, not from an outside service.

Permissions, and why

PermissionWhy it is needed
webNavigationTo detect when a tab moves to a new page, so the trail can be built.
tabsTo read each page's title and address, and to open an earlier step in the tab when you click it.
storageTo keep the trail in temporary storage and remember your settings.
faviconTo show each site's icon from the browser's local cache.

Your browser may describe these permissions as "read your browsing history". That wording reflects what the permissions allow; this extension uses them only as described here.

Your controls

  • Pause tracking stops recording new pages.
  • Exclude this site stops recording a site and removes it from trails already recorded. Excluded sites can be removed from the list at any time.
  • Clear trail and Clear all trails delete recorded trails immediately.
  • Removing the extension deletes all of its stored data.
  • The extension does not run in Incognito windows unless you allow that in your browser's extension settings.

Children

Trail Map is a general-purpose browsing tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it collects nothing.

Changes to this policy

If the extension's data handling ever changes, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before the new version is released.
